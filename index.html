The Eagle's Watch Project on GitHub

Follow every tax peso, from your barangay to Malacañang.

Taxes are earned through the blood and sweat of Filipino citizens. We are building a public watch on how that money is spent, from local governments up to the President.

Follow the project

In development. No figures are published yet.

Three levels we will watch

Barangays, municipalities, cities and provinces handle the projects closest to you: roads, clinics, schools and relief.

  • Annual budgets and what each project cost
  • Who won each contract
  • Whether the work was finished

National agencies and Congress decide how most of the country's money is allocated and released.

  • Agency budgets, year over year
  • Lawmakers' funded projects
  • Audit findings in plain language

The Office of the President controls large funds and sets spending priorities for the whole government.

  • Office budget and special funds
  • Major projects and their progress
  • Changes between proposal and final spending

Made for Filipino taxpayers. Guarding public money from LGUs up to the President.
